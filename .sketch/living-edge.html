How it comes together

From the footing up, each layer arrives in the order it is built. Each one has a job: carry, drain, separate, breathe, protect.

    What the weather does

    Rain arrives on the wind. The overhang shelters only the top of the wall. Below that the render takes the weather; water that gets behind it runs down the drained cavity and leaves over the flashing. Sun and wind dry it again.

    watersun

    Vancouver requires a drained cavity behind cladding (VBBL 9.27.2.2). A wall is treated as sheltered only within a quarter of the overhang's depth below it.

    Thirty years

    Where splash and shade keep the render damp, moss takes hold first. Lichen arrives after a decade or more. Roots find the drainage, the gravel silts, and the larch turns silver.

    Growth is shown in stages, not dates. Most of it rests on qualitative evidence.

    People and nature

    People sit where the wall stays dry. The seat wears pale where they rest, and moss keeps the corners. A careful clean resets the surface, for a while.
