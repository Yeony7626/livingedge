## What improved (agreed across reviewers)

- **Sill pan drains into the channel.** The lap-1 blocker is gone.
- **Slab edge.** Air, soil-gas and thermal continuity work: welded back leg, butyl-taped poly, cork strip, sealant.
- **Perimeter foundation drain.** Added, in a wrapped stone envelope with its own outlet.
- **Buried rainwater pipe replaced by a surface rill and runnel.** R2 and R3 call this the strongest move.
- **Rain garden.** 200 mm ponding, a lower far rim, the subdrain near the top of the rock, a beehive riser and planting proxies.
- **Eave and head.** R5: one charcoal band, a tucked head, a honey raked soffit and one cedar course "work".
- **Renders.** They can now judge colour and proportion.
- **Carbon accounting.** 8 % low, down from 27 % in lap 1.

## Problems, grouped (most raised by 2+ reviewers)

1. **The water chain does not drain at the bottom.**
   - The runnel floor ends at −222 against a rain-garden rim at −122: a 100 mm dam. Water stands in the rill and runnel. In design storms the backwater reaches the sill-pan nose (R3 N1 blocker, R2 N1).
   - The rill pierces the landing liner twice. The silt box cannot sit at the rill head (R2 N2).
   - Fix: runnel at about 2 % onto a stone lip and a cobble forebay. Move the rain chain, basin and rill **out of the opening, to a jamb** (R2, R3 P1, R5 P2 "water corner").
2. **The box gutter is drawn back to front.** Its house-side leg (3240) is 82 mm below the front lip (3322), so overflow goes behind the gutter into the PT sub-fascia (R5 N1, R3 N5). This is a modelling error.
3. **The threshold is 16 mm, not 13.** The new stainless rails stand 5 mm proud of the track (R1 N1, R3 N2). Lower the track so the rail top is at +5.
4. **Overflow hierarchy at the door.** The channel's only overflow (−6) is above the pan back-leg top (−15). A blocked channel floods towards the foundation joint (R2 N3, R1 N3). Fix: a bypass weir or notch at about −20 and the back leg raised to −5.
5. **Cold interior surfaces** (R1 2D screening, indicative):
   - Sill apron f_Rsi **0.65** and head plasterboard **0.67**, both under the 0.70 limit. At −7 °C outside that means condensation at about 54 % room RH.
   - Fix: a "warm sill" (load-bearing insulation replaces grout, wedge and apron; 4 mm metal floor plate) gives 0.92.
   - Fix: a two-piece head flashing, so the steel no longer touches the lintel, plus insulation in front of the frame head gives 0.84.
6. **Lintel.**
   - The W250x39 is not drawn at its real section or mass, and has no fixing grounds (R1 N5, R4 N3).
   - Steel costs about +40 kgCO2e/m against glulam, and the trade was not disclosed.
   - Alternatives: a flush glulam 175 × 494 with hung joists fits the existing zone (−39), or a reclaimed W-section (−48) (R4 P2).
7. **Glazing and frame procurement.**
   - The 4 / 1.3 / 4 thin-glass triple is not made at 2.4 × 2.6 m panes. Its carbon credit was not real (R4 N2).
   - A "fibreglass lift-and-slide" was not found in BC. The only BC-made PH lift-and-slide is PVC (R4 N11).
   - NAFS / A440 ratings are still unstated (R1 F14).
   - A product needs to be named before modelling further.
8. **Edges unresolved.** The jamb–sill corner (R1 N7), jamb base and ground (R5 N3, R3 N12), reveal returns and eave end (R5 N5, N9).
9. **West sun.** The overhang shades no glass below a 33.5° sun angle, so summer west sun from about 17:00 is unshaded. Integrate now: a dark metal reveal "picture frame" with a screen cassette in the empty head reveal (R5 N4, P3).
10. **Roots and tree pit.** The root enclosure around the lined landing is open in four places (R3 F7). The 1 m tree pit is an undrained sump in till (R3 N6, R2 N9).
11. **Operability.** A manual 300 kg lift-and-slide is not usable by wheelchair users. A motor drive is needed (R3 N3, R1 F20).
12. **Foundation.**
    - Deepen the beam to −530: drain cover 152 mm, frost margin 74 mm.
    - Track anchors would puncture the welded pan.
    - The pan and slab sequence cannot be followed as written. Split the pan: a cast-in back angle plus a welded pan fitted after framing (R2 N5, N6, N8; R1 N6).

The v2 gains (about −100: granite, pumice, HDPE, no spray foam) were spent by three swaps:
- steel lintel, +40;
- PU course, +25;
- stainless rill grate, +19.

R4's reductions:
- Stainless package: granite rill lids and roof-edge setts, low-carbon stainless elsewhere (−52).
- Lintel: glulam or reclaimed steel (−39 / −48).
- One reclaimed-granite ground (−43).
- Loose-laid EPDM under the green roof: no herbicide, no copper (−32).
- A right-sized PU course (−16).
- PVC out (−4).

Method issue (R4 N5): the T and H zone "kgCO2e/m²" divide a 2.7 m-tall door by a plan depth, which is not meaningful. That is a metrics.mjs fix.

## Where the v2 model or my disposition was wrong

- The gutter was drawn back to front (item 2).
- The stainless rails raised the threshold to 16 mm (item 3).
- The runnel outfall is dammed (item 1).
- The thin-glass triple was claimed as done but is not buildable.
- "Only proxies uncounted" was untrue (R4).
- The steel lintel's carbon trade was not disclosed.

## Decisions for the user before lap 3

1. **Water corner.** Move the rain chain, basin and rill from the middle of the opening to beside a jamb wall. R2, R3 and R5 all recommend it.
2. **Lintel.** Steel W250 (current), flush glulam with hung joists (−39, same depth) or reclaimed steel (−48).
3. **Frame.**
   - Name a product: Cascadia fibreglass non-lift PH slider, or a PH wood-aluminium lift-and-slide.
   - Westeck PVC is the only BC lift-and-slide found.
   - Add a motor drive.
4. **West shading now.** A screen cassette inside the head reveal plus a dark metal reveal frame (R5 P3).
5. **One ground.** Reclaimed granite throughout, coursed parallel to the glass (R5 P1, R4 P3).
