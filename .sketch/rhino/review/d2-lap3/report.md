## Drawing notes

- **beam**: Part 4 engineered foundation (R2 ruling on VBBL 9.15.4.6). Underside −530: frost margin 74, drain cover 152 (R2 N5). Sill support level ±1 mm over 4.8 m.
- **course**: Bedded on PU adhesive; drillable only below the pan line. Engineer to confirm long-term stress under the track (1.45 MPa grade).
- **dam-angle**: Interior water-barrier top −5 (5 mm sealant over): every exterior overflow crest is lower (notch −20, weir −95).
- **pan**: End dams 150 up the jambs, welded; jamb membranes lap ≥ 100 over them. Back upturn butyl-lapped to the dam angle (−60 → −25). No fastener through the pan floor (R2 N8).
- **stone**: Radon rough-in per VBBL 9.13.4.3 (outside the slice). Never linked to the perimeter drain.
- **sill-bearer**: On discrete 2–3 mm PU/epoxy pads at the fixing points; 5 x 3 grooves at 150 o.c. drain outward. Frame screws stop in the bearer (screw ≤ block + bearer − 3).
- **channel**: Opening width, 316 end caps flush with the reveal liners (R5 N3). Invert −95 → −120 toward the jamb-A outlet (0.5 %, not drawn); silt-box weir −95; bottom outlet to its own solid pipe (R2 N2, N3).
- **tray-stools**: Bonded on the pan floor over the channel house wall; nothing fixed through the pan.
- **glass-1**: 6/14 Ar/4/14 Ar/8.76 = 46.8 in the 48 rebate; Ug ≈ 0.55–0.6; ≈ 47 kg/m², sash ≈ 310 kg (≤ 400 kg drive). Dry-glazed for the year-30 IGU change.
- **door-unit**: ≈ 27 kgCO2e/m² of opening × 2.785 m (frame 15 + hardware 5 + drive 7, mid values, INFERRED); no product EPD — require EN 15804+A2 in the tender.
- **drive**: Dedicated 120 V circuit to the fixed-panel jamb; push buttons 900–1100 on both faces (B651 5.2.7.1); exterior button cable in a sealed grommet above the pan end dam.
- **glulam**: Stacked-stud / built-up header rejected: 30–40 mm deflection at this span (13 §3.2). Bears 114 on 3 jacks + 2 kings per side.
- **cassette**: 4.8 m needs a 140/180-class system (Renson Fixscreen 150 evo class, WK3). Guides inside the liners; bottom bar to the tray. Doubles as the insect screen when the wall is open.
- **canopy-cap**: Through-insulation sub-flash on a 6 % bevel in a horizontal WF joint at ≈ 2976, lapped under the WRB (FB23, not drawn).
- **liner**: Never pierced by surface water: the channel drains by its own solid pipe and the rill starts at jamb A outside the liner. w-end upturns wrap the channel end caps; the underdrain leaves through one welded boot in the w = 0 upturn (R2 N2, N4, N10).
- **underdrain**: R2 asked 1 %; 0.5 % fits the 30 mm trench over a 4.9 m run (INFERRED minimum for a perforated subdrain).
- **perim-drain**: Cleanouts at the corners; storm sump ≥ 750 deep with separate inlets for this drain and the underdrain (9.14.5 via 07 E10). Never linked to the channel, underdrain or sub-slab stone.
- **channel-pipe**: Channel catchment is the landing strip and glass wash only (roof water goes to the basin); 63 mm is INFERRED adequate — confirm with the channel maker.
- **basin-shell**: Inlet: the downspout from the roof above, spouting from the jamb-A wall. Overflow over the rim onto paving that falls away from the wall.
- **rg-riser**: Outlet to the tank / storm at invert ≤ PONDB − 785 with a backflow flap; 1 m spillway notch in the far rim at crest + 45 (outside this slice).
