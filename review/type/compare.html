Typeface candidates for Living Edge

Decision, 15 October 2026: 1 · DIN lineage. It is now applied to the landing page, the tool and the drawing labels. The tool's long part descriptions use regular Barlow; tags and drawing labels use Barlow Semi Condensed. Applied captures: hero, draft, thermal, phone.

The previous fonts, Geist and Geist Mono, had been picked in an earlier session and were never chosen by you. Each candidate below comes from a real lettering tradition. They are shown on the same three views, and only the fonts change. Click any image for full size.

0 · CURRENTGeist / Geist Mono / Barlow Condensed numeralsSoftware-UI fonts, common in AI-generated interfaces. The monospaced labels read as code, not drawing.
1 · DIN LINEAGEBarlow / Barlow Semi Condensed labels / Barlow Condensed numeralsDescends from DIN 1451, the German standard lettering for technical drawings and signage: even stroke width, simple grid, condensed labels. Reuses the numeral font already on the page.
2 · GROTESQUEArchivo / Archivo Narrow labels and numeralsA 19th-century American grotesque, close to the plain sans of architecture monographs and office sheet titles. Narrow labels pack tightly on the drawing.
3 · ENGINEERING SUPERFAMILYIBM Plex Sans / Plex Mono labels / Plex Sans Condensed numeralsOne family designed for engineering documentation. It keeps monospaced labels like the current look, but with more character.
