# Changes after the baseline user test (x0 → x1)

Baseline fun/wow (simulated user test): student 6, tutor 5, phone visitor 4 → **mean 5.0**. "Correct rather than fun"; the risk is dull, not juvenile.

## New interactions (research 19 proposals 1, 4)
- **Winter: drag the outside temperature.** The −7 °C in the sentence is a scrubbable number (drag sideways, arrow keys; −25 to +10 °C). The field keeps a fixed colour scale, so the cold creeps across the cut and the 0 °C isotherm moves; the coldest surfaces in the labels and stats update; f_Rsi stays 0.48 / 0.76 ("at any outside temperature"). The field is linear in the boundary temperatures, so this is exact, not an animation.
- **Ageing: a year-0 / year-N wipe** across the whole render. Drag the handle (or arrow keys); year 0 left, the chosen year right.
- **Phones and reduced motion: tap the model** to step Assembled → Taken apart → Winter night.

## Fixes from the user test
- **A year or stage the visitor picks holds** (slider, ticks, wipe, arrows, list, play) until they leave the band; scrolling no longer overrides it.
- **Phone explode** solved with 22 px gaps for a narrow stage (it rendered as five specks).
- **Phone controls under their figure:** the year, slider and ticks, the rain button, the stage number, name and stepper now come straight after the figure, which no longer sticks over them.
- **Rain** eases off over about two seconds after release.
- **Hero night held longer** (400 vh; night from 53 % to 93 % of the pin).
- **Tool:** a "Link" button copies a link to exactly this view (tab, cut, thermal case, conditions, part), and those links open it. NFRC runs explain why f_Rsi differs from the ISO 13788 check. The threshold dimension no longer appears on the jamb cut.
