**Compared with x0:** the right first moves. The temperature scrub is exact physics, and the held year and stage fix the scroll fights. The phone explode works and the tool has a Link button. But d01, d05 and d07 are pixel-identical to x0. On desktop the hero still does nothing under the hand, and the first ten seconds are the same as before. There is also a clarity slip: d06 says the head is 6.0 °C in the label and stats but 6.1 °C in "Why the head is cold".

## 2. Keep

- **The scrubbable −7 °C (d06).** A dashed red handle in the sentence, with f_Rsi holding "at any outside temperature". This is the Victor/Ciechanowski grammar done properly, and it is the tutor's lecture in one gesture.
- **The hero's three states (d02, d03).** The lettered H/D/T/F/M/E tags, the single red T line and the night flip are the most authored moments on the page, and they are serious, not cute.

## 3. Five changes, ranked

1. **Drag the cut: the signature peak (d01, right face of the section box, around 1370,540).** Add a short signal-red grip labelled "CUT W 2000". Dragging it moves `plane.constant` along W, with detents at jamb wall, water corner and sliding panel. Move the CPU caps with `toWorld` and rebuild varying parts with `sectionShapes(p, w)` only on a detent. The caption counts in Barlow tabular figures. Add an idle camera drift of at most 1.5° that follows the cursor, so the axo is never inert at rest. This one gesture also settles W 2000 versus w 900.
2. **Put the Winter input on the drawing (d06, m03).** The scrub sits in prose. It scrolls away (ut-student-2), and on the phone the model is off-screen while you drag it (m03). Do three things:
   - Mirror the scrub as a draggable bead on the legend ramp (d06, about 955,250–470).
   - Add the probe: a crosshair on the cut that samples the solved grid and writes "11.2 °C · f 0.68" with a `leader()`, red below 0.70.
   - Draw the 0 °C isotherm as an ink line that visibly travels as you drag.
   On phones, make the bead sticky under the figure.
3. **Make the ageing wipe impossible to miss (d04, m02).** The 22 px box at 474,396 sits on the glass, and the 0→30 difference in the main render is still faint. Use a full-height red hairline with Barlow "YR 0 | YR 30" tabs, parked over the cedar head. On band entry, sweep it once (about 900 ms) so the eye sees it is live. Push silvering and the splash band to their research-10 end values on the right side. Once the wipe carries the comparison, demote the three split bubbles to one.
4. **Turn the wind, and let the water arrive (d05, arrow at 647,253).** The rain is still faint grey hairlines. Make the wind arrow a slider (`uWind` into `rainU`) with a detent at the sourced design angle. Draw a dry shadow from the drip edge in the SVG. Reveal routes in order (glass → sill → trench), with each label inking in as the water reaches it.
5. **Design the ending, and carry the visitor's state into the tool (d08 tool tile at 1180–1385 × 640–810; m01).** The black TOOL tile is empty. Fill it with a live thumbnail of the Draft sheet at the visitor's own cut, year and temperature. Make the call to action "Take this sheet", and use the new Link parameters so the tool opens exactly there. On the phone, the hero model is still about 170 px with a 200 px void below (m01). Make it full width, with a one-line "tap the drawing" hint.
